## Direction contract

THESIS: kurcz.pl as the anatomy wall chart from a Polish biology classroom: a lithographed muscle plate with numbered callouts locates the cramp, and each guide is its own small chart. It refuses the stock-photo health portal and the cream antique plate book.

OWN-WORLD: deep slate green (#22302E) is the chart's cloth binding: a full-height side rail and chart-card title strips. The ground is cool linen grey-blue (#DCE3E4), never cream. Muscle is madder red (#A8432F) in the plates; ochre (#C9A24A) numbered callout discs and rail numerals. Engraved lithograph hatching lives only in the plates; the interface is crisp and flat, framed with thin slate rules. Sturdy humanist sans for headings, calm reading face for text.

STORY: the reader sees a familiar classroom chart, finds the leg where it hurts, follows the numbered steps in the rail, picks the chart card for their situation, and reads one calm column with the guide's own plate at its head.
